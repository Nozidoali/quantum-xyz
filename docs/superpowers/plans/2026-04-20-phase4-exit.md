## Design decisions made during execution

**Engine scheduling lives in `SynthesisEngine.run`, not in strategies.**
Strategies declare `applicable` and `synthesize`. The engine decides
whether to treat a strategy as short-circuit or competing. This keeps
strategy classes single-purpose.

**`enable_reindex` guard moved to engine.** The old `_prepare_state_rec`
had the raise in the middle of the body after a reindex_circuit call.
The engine puts the raise at the top of `run`, before any work. The
Phase 1 bug-6 test was adapted to check `stats.num_runs_support_reduction == 0`
after the raise (semantically equivalent to the old monkey-patched
spy approach — both verify no side effect before raise).

**`_prepare_state_rec` signature introspection dropped from tests.** The
Phase 1 test `test_prepare_state_default_stats_is_none` asserted that
both `prepare_state` AND `_prepare_state_rec` had `stats=None` as
default. `_prepare_state_rec` no longer exists, so the inner assertion
was removed. Outer (public) `prepare_state` still has the `stats=None`
default — that assertion stands.

**`num_reduced_supports` / `num_reduced_density` counters dropped.** The
old `_prepare_state_rec` updated these counters; the new engine does
not. No regression test depends on them. If someone relies on them
later, the engine can be extended.

**Stale `get_cnot_cost` missed in Phase 2.** The Phase 2 sweep had left
a `gate.get_cnot_cost()` call in
`tests/test_algorithms/test_exact_cnot_synthesis/test_state_transtition.py`.
Commit `e627fa4` fixed it as a Phase 4 leftover.
